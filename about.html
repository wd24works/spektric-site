About Spektric

We understand behavior. We understand people. We build care around both.

Spektric was founded to bring behavioral, psychological, and neurodevelopmental expertise together — in one practice, around one person at a time.

Mission

Meaningful skills. Greater independence. A better life.

To help individuals develop meaningful skills, stronger relationships, and a better quality of life — through care that is individualized, evidence-informed, and deeply respectful of the person.

Vision

Every person seen whole.

A behavioral healthcare experience where every person is seen as a whole person, every plan is built around a real life, and progress is measured by what matters.

The name

Why “Spektric.”

A spectrum is not a line from less to more. It is a full range of strengths, needs, abilities, and possibilities, unique to each person. Our name — and the spectrum line that runs through this site — is a reminder to look at the whole range.

Clinical approach

How we practice.

Four commitments hold across every service and every clinician.

Individualized

Assessment comes before intervention. Goals are written with the person and family and reflect the settings where skills must actually work.

Evidence-informed

We use approaches grounded in established behavioral and psychological science — and we measure whether they are working for this person.

Collaborative

Families, caregivers, schools, and physicians are partners. With consent, we coordinate so that care is coherent across settings.

Ethical

We recommend only what we believe will help, we say so when something else would serve you better, and we protect privacy and dignity at every step.

Leadership

The people behind the practice.

Clinical, operational, and compliance leadership under one roof.

Alina Vitali

MSW, LMHC

Chief Executive Officer

As CEO of Spektric LLC, Alina Vitali brings extensive clinical leadership, behavioral health expertise, and a strong commitment to building innovative, compassionate, and outcomes-driven services in ABA and mental health care.

As a Florida Licensed Mental Health Counselor and experienced Clinical Director, she has led multidisciplinary teams, developed and implemented evidence-informed treatment programs, strengthened clinical and documentation standards, and managed complex behavioral-health populations with significant mental-health and substance-use needs.

Her approach combines clinical excellence with practical leadership, operational discipline, and a belief that effective care should be individualized, measurable, and accessible.

Through Spektric, she is focused on creating a modern behavioral-health organization where high-quality clinical services, technology, and thoughtful systems come together to produce meaningful outcomes for clients and families.

Sheryl Mayoz

RN, MBA, LHRM, CHC, CCA

Chief Financial Officer

Sheryl has over 30 years of experience in the for-profit, not-for-profit, and public health care arena. She has provided operational and regulatory guidance to health care partners in large healthcare systems, behavioral health centers/settings, hospitals, health plans, medical schools, residency programs, community-based outreach programs, inpatient rehab facilities, skilled nursing units, wound care centers, home health agencies, and physician practice groups.

Relevant Experience:

Sheryl has a keen knowledge base in behavioral health compliance, internal audit, healthcare operations, physician practice and contract management, risk management, case management, quality management/PI, coding/billing/reimbursement, and customer service initiatives.

Specialties include developing and sustaining comprehensive programs in alignment with State and Federal laws, developing and monitoring healthcare audit programs, contract development, clinical documentation improvement, accreditation standards, Medicaid, Medicare, commercial/private pay industry managed care/ Medicare + Choice, patient centered care models, and population health.

Sheryl is a FL licensed registered nurse, FL licensed healthcare risk manager, FL licensed certified claims adjuster, and a nationally certified healthcare compliance / privacy officer. She received her undergrad from Nova Southeastern University, and her MBA and MHA from the New York Institute of Technology.
